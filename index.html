Field note 01 / The shape of home

Walk the paths.
Learn the names.

Explore a deterministic, hand-shaped countryside rendered in original pixel forms. Roads stay on the grid. Buildings carry their own silhouettes. Six residents give the settlement a pulse before the wider world opens beyond it.

This is the beginning of a larger desktop game: a stronger visual foundation first, then interiors, routines, craft, weather, and the stories that grow from staying awhile.
